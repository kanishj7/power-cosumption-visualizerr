Energy Saving Tips

  • Set your AC to 24–26 °C and clean its filters monthly.
  • Switch to LED bulbs and turn off lights in empty rooms.
  • Unplug chargers and appliances on standby.
  • Run washing machines with full loads.
  • Choose 4- or 5-star rated appliances when replacing old ones.
  • Lower your water heater's thermostat and use it only when needed.
